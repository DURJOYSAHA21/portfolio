About me

The short version of who I am and how I work.

Final-year CSE student in Dhaka, building the kind of machine learning that can explain itself.

The way I work is unglamorous. A question becomes a notebook, the notebook becomes a repo, and the repo becomes something a person can actually click. I would rather ship a rough version that works this week than a perfect plan that never leaves the doc.

It started as web development — complex designs, critical situations, things that had to work in front of users. It ended up in research papers on deepfake detection and chatbots for mental health. Same job either way: build something that holds up when a person depends on it.

    SkillHub

    A full-stack learning marketplace: mentors publish courses, learners find, buy and finish them in their own language, and an admin approves courses and runs the platform.     2026

    JobMatch-BD

    Upload a CV, or fill in a short profile, and JobMatch BD ranks tech jobs in Bangladesh against it. Every result shows why it matched: the skills you have, the ones you're missing, and a score breakdown. The score combines semantic similarity (sentence embeddings, so “neural networks” can match “deep learning”) with structured matching of skills, education, experience and location. It fetches live postings, keeps a shortlist, and can email a digest when new jobs score at least 50. The API runs on a free server, so the first request after a quiet spell can take about a minute.

  • Course project 11 screenshots

    2026

    Luffy — The Unknown Journey

    A 2D story adventure for our Computer Graphics course: a boy named Luffy is pulled into the World of Chaos through his game console and has to cross five levels, each with a different kind of gameplay, to beat the Chaos King and get home. Play it in the browser. Everything on screen is drawn with OpenGL shapes and every sound is generated in code: no images, sprites, sound files or game engine. It runs in the browser through WebAssembly. Techniques include particle systems, a radial torch-light mask, hierarchical modelling, projectile physics, and hand-written DDA line drawing and Cohen–Sutherland clipping. A five-person team. I made the story, the title screen and cutscenes, and Level 1, the dungeon. The repository holds the playable build; the source code is private. Fan project: the hero is a tribute to Luffy from One Piece (Eiichiro Oda).

    Feb — May 2025

    Zoo Management System

    A desktop app that runs a whole zoo, built for the OOP-II course in C# WinForms on SQL Server: 47 screens over a 27-table database, with separate sides for admins, staff, doctors and visitors. Visitors sign up with an email OTP, buy tickets and memberships, book events, shop, and pay through a built-in bank system that confirms each transaction with a one-time code. Staff get work schedules, their assigned animals and a live chat. Doctors run checkups, treatments and medical records. Admins manage animals, employees, members, visitors, events and status reports. There is also a map page for getting to the zoo and a video tour. The point was to make the object model visible in something that actually runs.
